# Changelog

## 1.0.2 (2026-10-12)

- Fixed a crash on startup caused by Botania reading its client config before it was loaded (issue #6).
- Compatibility with Spectrum: its black lotus items work in mana pools.
- New Helms of Revealing (manasteel, elementium and terrasteel) when Thaumaturge is installed.
- The Ponder integration accepts Ponder 1.0.82 or newer.
- Loonium drops dungeon loot directly by default, with the mob mode still available in the config.
- New modules: Exploration, Creative Crafting and Asgard, and the Skybox module (disabled by default).

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-04)

- Version bump and Ponder dependency range fix.

## 1.0.0

- First release: Botania ported to Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge.
